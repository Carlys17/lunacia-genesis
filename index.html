How to Play

🎯 First play — 60 seconds

  1. You start with 2 starter Axies, 1000 SLP, and both breed slots pre-filled.
  2. Hit BREED — watch the gene roll — a newborn Axie joins your collection.
  3. Send to Arena from the reveal, or pick any Axie in the Arena tab.
  4. Win gauntlet fights: +3 🏆 +15 SLP each, sweep bonus +10 🏆 +50 SLP.
  5. Breed smarter each generation: same-class parts stack power, purity drives rarity.

🧬 Genetics rules

70%dominant gene (d1) from a random parent
30%recessive gene (d2) surfaces instead
3%mutation: brand-new part appears
  • Offspring class: 50/50 from either parent.
  • Stats average both parents with natural variance.
  • Purity = parts matching the Axie's class. 6/6 pure parts = +36 power and top rarity odds.
  • Bloodline block: siblings and parent-child pairs can never breed.
  • Every Axie carries hidden recessive genes (d2) — two ordinary parents can hide a champion.

⚔️ Arena — Manual Combat

Turn-based card combat, 5 fights. Your Axie's 6 body parts are its cards — each costs 1-3 ⚡ energy and you refuel 3 every round. Click a card to play it, then the foe strikes back. Win = +3 🏆 +SLP; losing costs nothing — free practice.

Card types: ⚔️ atk damage · 🛡️ shield absorbs incoming hits · ✨ buff raises attack · 🎯 crit boosts crit chance. Skill drives damage, Morale crits (1.7×), Speed never matters in card duels — pick wisely. ⚡ Auto button plays for you.

🌀 Class Counter

Axie classes form an advantage wheel: Beast > Bug, Reptile · Bug > Plant, Reptile · Bird > Plant, Aquatic · Plant > Aquatic, Bird · Aquatic > Beast, Bug · Reptile > Bird, Beast. Landing a class advantage deals 1.15× damage (▲ADV), a disadvantage deals 0.85× (▼DIS). Check the foe's class before you commit!

⭐ Rarity

Score = power + purity × 15. Common < 300 · Rare < 340 · Epic < 380 · Legendary ≥ 380. Release Axies you don't need for SLP based on power.
