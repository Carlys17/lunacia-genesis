How to Play

First play

  1. You start with 2 starter Axies and 1000 SLP.
  2. Click an Axie in your collection → loads into Slot A. Click another → Slot B. Clicking the same Axie twice cycles its slot.
  3. Press BREED and watch the gene roll. Costs SLP.
  4. The newborn joins your collection — then Send to Arena to fight 5 opponents and win trophies + SLP.
  5. Rinse, repeat, climb the leaderboard. Bred Axies with pure matching parts (same class) are stronger in the Arena.

Genetics rules

70%dominant gene (d1) from a random parent
30%recessive gene (d2) surfaces
3%mutation: a brand-new part appears
  • Offspring class: 50% from either parent.
  • Siblings can never breed — two Axies sharing a parent are bloodline-blocked.
  • Each Axie's breed cost grows with its own breed count.
  • Stats (HP, Speed, Skill, Morale) average the parents with natural variance.

Arena

Auto-battle vs 5 procedurally generated challengers. Higher Speed strikes first each round, Skill boosts damage, Morale adds crits, HP keeps you standing. Each win: +3 trophies, +15 SLP.

Goal

Breed the highest-power champion, stack trophies, and claim the top of the Lunacia Genesis leaderboard.
